Biography

Hi! I am Vu Dinh Ngoc Bao, a senior student in the Bachelor of Information Technology (Honors / Talented Program) at the University of Science, VNU-HCM (HCMUS) with a cumulative GPA of 3.81/4.0. My work focuses on Cloud-Native Infrastructure (Kubernetes, AWS, GCP), CI/CD Pipeline Security (DevSecOps), Privacy-Preserving AI / Graph RAG, and Scalable Backend Systems (Go, Python).

I am the first author of two research papers submitted to ACIIDS 2027 (investigating patient identifier leakage in Medical Graph RAG at SELab — HCMUS) and IEEE ICC 2027 (measuring CI/CD egress-allowlist reachability during my research internship at the Cyber Information Security Lab — National Chung Cheng University, Taiwan). Previously, I worked as a Backend Developer Intern at BAO Insure and was a Cloud Track Trainee at Viettel Digital Talent. Outside the lab, I enjoy competing in hackathons and sharing tech/research stories on TikTok (@banana_dancing_in_rain).

    Graph-Derived Controls for Patient Identifier Leakage in Medical Graph RAG

    Role: First Author · Lab: SELab — University of Science, VNU-HCM · Venue: ACIIDS 2027

    Measured stage-by-stage PII leakage across LightRAG and Plain RAG on medical dialogues using synthetic canary patients. Designed P2q (graph-derived person dictionary + age coarsening, filtering contexts in 33 ms) and P4 (provenance document pruning + per-query ID hashing), reducing patient-diagnosis linkage and cited-ID join attacks to 0%.

    Graph RAGLightRAGPrivacy-Preserving AIACIIDS 2027

    EgressGap — Measuring Egress-Allowlist Reachability in GitHub Actions

    Role: First Author · Lab: Cyber Information Security Lab, CCU (Taiwan) · github.com/chuitrai/EgressGap

    Analyzed 7,137 enforcing Harden-Runner allowlists across 1,358 public GitHub Actions repositories by fusing workflow static rules, IP co-tenancy/abuse catalogs, and public CI DNS logs. Demonstrated that 56.7% of policies admit co-tenant or exfiltration channels and static tightening breaks 98.4% of builds (submitted to IEEE ICC 2027).
